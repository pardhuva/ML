
//Regularization 
 Regularization is used to prevent overfitting.

 overfitting happens when :
  . model is too complex 
  . fits noise instead of the real patter 
  . performs well on training data but poorly on test data 

Goal: Make the model simpler so it generalizes well.

//bias and variance 
 These are two types of errors in any model.
 1. bias 
   Error from wrong assumptions.
   ex: data is quadratic, you fit straight line 
    high bias model - too simple 
    outcome :
      underfitting 
      makes saem mistakes on every dataset 

 2. variance 
   Error from being too sensitive to training data.
   Fit an 8th-degree polynomial
   - high variance model - too complex 
   outcome :
     overfitting 
     training error extremely low 
     test error veyr high
